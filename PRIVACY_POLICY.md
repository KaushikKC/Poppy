# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 1, 2026**

Poppy ("Poppy", "the app", "we", "us") is a personal AI voice companion. This policy
explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **Poppy runs
entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal
data.** There are no accounts, no cloud servers, and no third-party AI services.

If anything here is unclear, contact us at **poppysdotsocial@gmail.com**.

---

## The one-line summary

Everything you say to Poppy, everything she remembers, and every conversation stays
**on your own device**. It never leaves your machine, and we never see it.

## What Poppy processes, and where

All of the following happens **locally on your device**. None of it is sent to us or to
any third party.

- **Your voice (microphone).** When you are on a call, Poppy uses your microphone to hear
  you. The audio is converted to text **on your device** and is not recorded, stored, or
  uploaded. It is discarded after it is transcribed.
- **Your messages and Poppy's replies.** The conversation is generated by an AI model that
  runs **on your device**. Nothing is sent to an external server to produce a reply.
- **Poppy's voice.** Her spoken replies are synthesized **on your device**.
- **Conversation history.** Your chats are stored **locally on your device** so the app
  works and you can review them. They are not uploaded.
- **Memory.** With your explicit consent, Poppy remembers facts you choose to save. These
  memories are stored **locally and encrypted** on your device (using industry-standard
  encryption). Each character keeps its own separate memory. You can view, edit, or delete
  any memory, or clear all of them, at any time in the app.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** collect or receive any of your data.
- We do **not** transmit your voice, conversations, or memories off your device.
- We do **not** use accounts or logins.
- We do **not** use third-party AI services or share data with them.
- We do **not** use advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.
- We do **not** sell or rent your information to anyone.

## App analytics

Poppy may keep simple, **content-free** usage counts **on your device** (for example, "a
call happened") to power in-app features like your streak. These counts contain **no
conversation content and no personal information**, and they are **not transmitted** off
your device.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Microphone.** Used only during a call, only to hear you, and only on your device, as
  described above. You can revoke this permission in your system settings at any time.
- **Notifications** (optional). If you opt into a daily ritual reminder, the app schedules a
  **local** notification on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

## Your control over your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in control:

- **Review** everything Poppy remembers in the app.
- **Edit or delete** any individual memory.
- **Clear** all memories or your conversation history at any time.
- **Delete everything** by removing the app; this deletes all local Poppy data on that
  device.

## Security

Saved memories are encrypted at rest on your device with industry-standard encryption, and
the encryption key is stored locally and readable only by your user account. This is privacy
hygiene for an on-device app. As with any software, please keep your device and operating
system secure and up to date.

## Children

Poppy is not directed to children. Please do not use the app if you are under the age
required by the app's App Store age rating in your region.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your information, we will update this policy and change the
effective date above. If a future version of the app ever adds an optional feature that
sends any data off your device, we will disclose it clearly and ask for your consent before
it is used.
