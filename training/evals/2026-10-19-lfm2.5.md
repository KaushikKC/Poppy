# LFM2.5-1.2B vs the shipping Qwen3 0.6B (v7a)

19 October 2026. Can Liquid's LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct replace Qwen3 0.6B as the base?

## Setup

- **Models.** v7a (shipping, Qwen3 0.6B fine-tune), stock LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct, and
  LFM2.5-1.2B fine-tuned on the v8 dataset ("lfm-tuned-v1"). All Q4_K_M GGUF on llama-server.
- **Replay.** `training/eval_replay.py`, phone-like (penalty off, two-sentence cap, the
  phone's repeat filter). 4 characters x 2 conversations (the Luna tester conversation,
  and the "wbu / what about u" one) x 3 runs = 252 turns per model.
- **Training.** Same as v8: `train.sh` with `BASE=LiquidAI/LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct`, 1400
  iterations fresh, LoRA on 16 layers, lr 1e-5, batch 2. About 2 hours on the M3 at
  0.2 it/s, peak 4.1 GB. Validation loss 2.50, then 1.64 at 200, 1.52 at 400, and 1.48 at 1400.
  Merged with `training/fuse_lfm.py` because `mlx_lm fuse` breaks LFM2.

## Results

| | v7a (shipping) | LFM2.5 stock | LFM2.5 tuned |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repeats an earlier line, before the filter | 34% | 1% | 12% |
| Repeats that got past the filter | 31 | 0 | 8 |
| Answers about herself when asked | 82/84 | 66/84 | 79/84 |
| Filler replies | 11 | 20 | 4 |
| Em dashes | 0 | 22 | 0 |
| Invented details (heuristic) | 3% | 0% | 7% |
| Download | 397 MB | 731 MB | 731 MB |

Liquid's recommended sampling (temperature 0.1) did worse on the stock model than the
phone's: 6% repeats, 60/84 about herself, 25 filler.

## Reading the transcripts

- **Tuned LFM is the most coherent of the three.** It answers "wbu" from her actual life
  (Biscuit, the flower orders, the swim before the show, Second Breakfast) and stays on
  topic. v7a drifts into lines like "You've got me on my own, Kaushik".
- **Stock LFM** barely repeats itself, but it deflects more, reads like an assistant ("You're
  welcome!" when nobody said thanks), says "Morning" on a Tuesday afternoon, and uses em dashes.
- **Tuned LFM invents real places** in her city (Barton Creek for Zoe in Austin, the
  Willamette for Poppy) and once a "mom". Most of the heuristic's hits are these, plus false
  positives like "Friday" and "Italian".
- **Fine-tuning raised repetition** from 1% to 12%. The v8 data says the same few life lines
  over and over ("just got back from a swim"), and the model learned that. This is a data
  problem, not a base-model one.

## Verdict and next steps

LFM2.5-1.2B tuned beats v7a on repetition, filler and coherence, and ties it on answering
about herself. It costs 330 MB more download. llama.rn 0.12.6 already supports the LFM2
architecture.

1. Phone test with the LAN loop: reply latency, memory, the first download, and whether the
   phone's filter catches the 12%.
2. If it holds, lfm-v2: vary how each character talks about her day, and filter invented
   places and family out of the training data.
3. Ship as a build with that one change.
